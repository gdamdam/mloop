Sample. Loop. Now.

mloop is a fast live sampler and looper. Record audio directly from your mic, layer loops, play pads, sequence a kit — all in the browser, all on your device.

Works on iPhone, iPad, Android, desktop No cloud — your audio stays local Export WAV or session JSON
Open mloop Source on GitHub Companion: mpump
pad sampler + loop station + step sequencer + 9 effects + kaos pad + destruction mode
Zero friction start No sign-up screen. No project setup. Grant mic access and you're recording.
Two modes, one session PAD and LOOPER share the same AudioContext and persist together — switch modes without losing work.
Your audio stays local Sessions are stored in your browser's IndexedDB. No data ever leaves your device.
Export when you're ready Mix down all loops to a timestamped WAV or export the session JSON to reload anywhere.
Why It Feels Different
No signup. No loading. Just record.

Many browser tools front-load sign-up screens before you can make any sound. mloop skips that — open the page, grant mic access, and start.

Instant live input

Grant mic access once and record loops or pads directly from any input connected to your device.

PAD + LOOPER in one session

Both modes share the same AudioContext. Switch between them freely — your loops and pads persist together.

Save, export, or share

Name and pin sessions in the browser, export a WAV mixdown, or share effects settings as a URL.

PAD
MPC-style sampler in the browser.

16 pads, each with its own sample, trim, pitch, pan, and play mode.

Record or import any sample Capture audio live from mic, resample from the looper, or import a file.
Step sequencer 8 / 16 / 32 / 64 steps with swing, real-time step recording, and mute groups.
7 built-in drum kits Acoustic, lo-fi, electronic, percussion — load a kit and you're playing in one tap.
Sample slicer + chromatic Slice a loop into pads automatically, or use pads as a chromatic instrument.
LOOPER
Live loop station with effects.

3 independent tracks, each with layered overdubs, sync, and a full effects chain.

Record · Overdub · Undo Layer takes non-destructively. One tap undo removes the last overdub layer.
3 sync modes FREE for freeform ambient, SYNC for phase-locked looping, LOCK for a fixed time window.
KAOS XY pad + 9 effects Delay, reverb, distortion, chorus, flanger, phaser, bitcrush, compressor, tremolo — controllable with gestures.
Destruction mode Progressive tape degradation per cycle — pitch drift, wow & flutter, bit reduction.
Privacy
Your music never leaves your device.

mloop processes audio locally using the Web Audio API. No audio is ever uploaded. No account is ever required. Microphone audio never leaves your device.

No cookies or accountsNo sign-up, no login, no session tokens.
No third-party trackersNo Google, no Facebook, no ad networks. Visit counts are anonymized via GoatCounter.
Local storage onlySessions, kits, and settings live in your browser's IndexedDB. Export to move them.
No fingerprintingNo device or browser identification of any kind.
Open sourceAll code is public on GitHub — audit it yourself.
